# Hello World!

Welcome to the new **KoalaStuff** blog. We finally moved to our own static `/blog` setup powered by Astro Content Collections and MDX.

This allows us to write deep dives, share updates, and host everything natively on our own domain `koalastuff.net` without relying on third-party tracking or bloated CMS systems.

> Everything here is pre-rendered to 100% static HTML.

## Why MDX?

With MDX, we can even embed interactive components right into our posts. 
For example, we could drop a native `ProjectCard` right here!

Stay tuned for more updates.
