Welcome to the brand new home of KoalaStuff! 

For a long time, all of our projects and browser extensions lived on their own separate subdomains. While this kept things nicely isolated from a development perspective, it became increasingly frustrating to manage from an SEO perspective.

### The Search Console Nightmare

The main reason for this change comes down to how Google Search Console and Bing Webmaster Tools handle subdomains versus main domains. They strongly prefer a single, authoritative main domain when ranking pages.

It became almost impossible to properly verify and display subdomains nicely, especially when trying to link out to the Chrome Web Store extension pages for projects like KoalaSync. The SEO tools were practically begging for a centralized hub.

### A Beautiful New Home

Because of these technical necessities, I decided to build a beautiful, fast, and centralized landing page. 

Here you'll find:
- A complete tracker of all our projects (Web, Desktop, and Extensions).
- Real-time updates directly fetched from GitHub.
- Our new static blog for deep-dives and development logs.

This change not only makes the SEO gods happy but also gives you a much better overview of everything we are working on.

Stay tuned for more updates!
